About these guides. Manufacturers revise their manuals over the years, and a manual sometimes disagrees with itself or with the maker's website. We report what is printed and posted: where two figures differ, we show both right there, with links to the pages, and where the maker's website gives a different figure, we note it next to the manual's. Plain misprints, such as a metric conversion that doesn't match, are corrected with a small note saying what was printed. The official manual is the authority for installation; when two figures differ, ask us or your installer before relying on either.
