Gas pressure faults

⚠ Insufficient pressure

  • Small pilot flame → weak millivolt signal and burner outages
  • Little change in flame between HI and LO on the regulator knob
  • Undersized supply feeding several appliances → nuisance dropouts or surges

⚠ Excessive pressure

  • Permanent valve damage → full appliance shutdown
  • Oversized pilot flame overheats the sensor → shutdown
  • Sooting and white residue from flame impingement / wrong fuel-air mix
