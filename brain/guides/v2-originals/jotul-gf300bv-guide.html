Field faults

⚠ Pilot won't stay lit

  • Pilot doesn't engulf the top 3/8" of the thermopile → weak millivolt, burner drops out
  • Dirty or corroded thermopile / thermocouple, or a loose TH/TP connection
  • Pilot orifice blocked, or the wrong size after a fuel conversion
  • Embers or debris covering the pilot carry-over ports

⚠ Spill switch tripping / draft

  • Blocked or disassembled vent, or an obstructed termination cap
  • Down-draft or flow reversal — common on outside or basement chimneys
  • House depressurization competing with the atmospheric B-vent
  • Vent under 7 ft / over 35 ft, or more than 4 ft of horizontal run
