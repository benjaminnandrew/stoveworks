Gas pressure faults

⚠ Insufficient pressure

  • Small pilot flame → poor rectification and burner outages
  • Little change in flame between HI and LO
  • Not enough gas for multiple appliances → nuisance outages or surges

⚠ Excessive pressure

  • Permanent valve damage → full appliance shutdown
  • Oversized pilot flame overheats the sensor → shutdown
  • Sooting from flame impingement / wrong fuel-air mix
